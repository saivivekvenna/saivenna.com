THE REAL ROOMS, RECONSTRUCTED

A closer Louvre.

Walk through Salle Mollien (700), Salon Denon (701), Salle Daru (702), and Salle des États (711). Their proportions and connections follow the Louvre’s published first-floor plan. All installed paintings use their original dimensions.

The Mona Lisa occupies its separate central display wall, facing The Wedding at Cana. The red galleries use the museum’s room records and photographs, including a dated 2016 view of Salle Daru.

Room assignments are verified; some wall positions and hanging heights are approximate. Architecture and decoration are modeled interpretations, not a laser survey or a claim to reproduce today’s complete hanging. Each painting identifies its placement evidence.
