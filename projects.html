›Swing Around – YC Hackathon
May 2026

An event networking layer for rooms where the signal is there but the structure isn't. Instead of scrolling past everyone in one giant chat thread, you drop a 90-second voice memo about who you are, what you're looking for, and what you can offer. GBrain indexes it as a living profile and surfaces the people in the room you should actually meet. We ran it live at the YC hackathon: interviewed 50 people, fed every profile in, and let the system build the real graph of who should meet who. Founders raising matched to VCs writing checks at their stage, engineers stuck on infra matched to people who'd shipped the same thing. Works for any event with a guest list, and the bigger the room, the more it matters.
