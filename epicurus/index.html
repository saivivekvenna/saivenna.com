Epicurus

01 biomedical hypothesis discovery

Epicurus gives researchers the reach of a thousand postdocs: a human-directed AI system that can autonomously generate novel biomedical hypotheses, test them in silico and in the wet lab, and iterate from the results. It uses multi-agent reasoning over a biomedical knowledge graph to support hypothesis generation, target discovery, and evidence-backed scientific discovery workflows.
